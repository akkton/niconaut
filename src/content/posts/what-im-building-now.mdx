My current work centers on GMT and the personal agent systems I use alongside product delivery. Lydar is in testing, and OneProposal is parked. Here is where the work stands in October 2026.

## GMT

GMT is an insurance software business I co-founded with partners who bring the operating experience. I lead product and technical delivery: turning workflow problems into requirements, making development decisions, reviewing the implementation and testing the result.

The product brings lead preparation, CRM integration and calling into one workspace for Sales and Recruiting. A large part of the work has been keeping original source records intact while letting an operator prepare a list, make corrections and save outcomes without losing their place.

GMT is still in development. Some modules are usable in the invitation-only application; others remain demonstrations or plans. I use controlled calling tests, saved-data checks and interruption recovery to decide which changes are ready to move forward.

[GMT's public site](https://globe-multi-tool.com/) explains the scope.

## AI in the delivery process

I use ChatGPT, Claude and Grok for research, analysis and reasoning through decisions. Cursor, Claude Code, Codex and Lovable assist implementation.

I define the requirements, provide the relevant context, review the changes and test the behavior. In GMT, that means checking saved results, integration boundaries and recovery alongside the interface. I decide whether the result meets the requirement and is ready to be accepted.

## Personal agent systems

I self-hosted OpenClaw as a personal assistant, with persistent memory, reviewed meeting intake, task tracking and health monitoring.

One useful failure was in the backups. A backup job could report completion even when the latest changes had not reached the remote repository. I added revision and freshness checks so that the status reflected the saved result, and tested outage and recovery alerts.

With Hermes and Paperclip, I have explored delegation, orchestration and human review. That work includes local rehearsals and experiments, alongside the implemented personal tools.

## Lydar and OneProposal

[Lydar](https://app.lydar.com.br/en) is a Brazilian home-services marketplace with client and professional workflows. It is still in testing. The current product lets the two sides arrange payment directly; it does not process their payments.

[OneProposal](https://oneproposal.app/) is parked. I built a proposal-generation and refinement product, including a hosted output page and the failure handling around generation. The [public sample](https://oneproposal.app/p/demo) illustrates the output format.
