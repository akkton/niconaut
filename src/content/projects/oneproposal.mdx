I built OneProposal to turn a freelance project brief and background information into a proposal and a hosted page. The product is parked. The public sample preserves the output format.

## What it actually is

I built a proposal-generation and refinement product with a front end and a separate generation engine. The work covered research, extraction, a cover letter, a hosted proposal page and checks on the generated output. It also included the contracts and callbacks connecting the two parts, plus recovery when a request failed.

## The hard parts

One recurring problem was contract drift. The engine could return a field the front end did not use, leaving a feature partly implemented. I used explicit request and callback contracts and regression checks to make that boundary easier to verify.

Owner tests exercised the payment and generation paths. Customer validation required a separate acquisition step. The acquisition sprint ended without reaching its goal, and I parked the project.
