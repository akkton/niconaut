I self-hosted OpenClaw as a personal assistant with persistent memory, reviewed meeting intake, task tracking and health monitoring. I use these systems to retain context between sessions and keep work reviewable.

## What the work taught me

A backup job could report completion while its latest changes had not reached the remote repository. I added revision and freshness checks and tested outage and recovery alerts. That work made monitoring a check of the saved result.

## What remains experimental

With Hermes and Paperclip, I have explored delegation, shared context and review of completed work. I tested parts of the process in an isolated local rehearsal. The complete hosted workflow remains experimental.
