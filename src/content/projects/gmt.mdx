GMT is an insurance software business I co-founded with partners who bring insurance operating experience. I lead product and technical delivery, including requirements, development decisions, review and testing.

## The working problem

An operator needs to bring source leads into a usable list, review the original context, call and save the result. Those steps have to work together. Keeping the original CRM record intact while saving corrections and outcomes separately has shaped much of the design.

## How I work on it

I use AI tools to assist research and implementation. I define what the operation needs to do, review the changes and test the behavior, including saved data and recovery after interruptions. My responsibility is deciding whether the result meets the requirement.

## Current status

GMT is still in development. Parts of the Sales workflow are available in the invitation-only application; other modules remain demonstrations or plans. I have run controlled calling and recovery tests. Broader operator acceptance and customer rollout remain ahead.
